Nearby networks

The network name and password are sent to the smoker over Bluetooth and stored on the ESP32. Use a 2.4 GHz network; the ESP32 can't join 5 GHz.

Firmware update

Opens the smoker's update page, where you upload a compiled .bin. Your phone must be on the same WiFi network as the smoker. It asks for the update username and password you set in the sketch.
